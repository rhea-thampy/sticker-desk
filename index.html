Sticker desk

Peel a sticker from its edge, carry it around and let go to stick it down. Make your own from a photo, give it a finish (matte, gloss, laminated, holographic or glitter), then download it, or arrange your stickers and save them as a wallpaper.

Mac app

Stickers on your real desktop

The free Sticker Desk app puts your stickers right on your Mac's desktop, or on top of your windows. Make them from photos, pick a finish and a size, and peel them around whenever you like.

Download for Mac
Free · macOS 13 or later · Apple silicon and Intel · Windows version coming
Opening it the first time
  1. Unzip the download and drag Sticker Desk into Applications.
  2. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer, because this is an indie app that isn't registered with Apple. Click Done.
  3. Go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to Sticker Desk.
  4. That's it: it opens normally from then on.
